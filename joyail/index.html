How it works

Every hour, each guard gets a quick call.

The same simple check, all night. It takes each guard about ten seconds.

In your own voice

Your guards hear you — not a robot.

We record a short sample of you speaking, once. From then on, every check-in call is in your own voice: familiar, personal, exactly how you'd say it yourself.

So the system doesn't give up after one ring — and if a guard is slow to answer, it checks where they are.
